## Main problems, ranked by damage

**1. The abstract claims more than the paper proves.**
- Abstract and intro say the serializer "partitioned a benchmark into a solvable and an unsolvable half." Stated as fact.
- Section VI says this is only correlational. The repaired agent also fails on E1c. The hard half was "plausibly capability and delivery jointly."
- A strict reviewer reads both and cuts the rigour score.
- Fix: soften the abstract and intro. Say the split "lined up with" the delivery defect. Make the proven result the headline: the destruction is deterministic, the harm depends on redundancy, and the cost shows up as tokens before errors.

**2. Two of your four hypotheses have no results.**
- H3 (removing S4 shifts failures to false negatives) and H4 (evidence yield predicts accuracy better than utilisation) are registered. Results are never reported.
- Reviewers will ask: did they fail, or did you not run them?
- Fix: report them, even in one line each. If they are pending, say so clearly and move them to Future Plans. Do not leave them silent.

**3. The instrument is proposed but never used.**
- Contribution 2 is the "attrition profile" and "evidence yield." You never show one profile. You never give one evidence yield number.

**4. "Defect class" rests on one harness.**
