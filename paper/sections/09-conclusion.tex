\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked how much an LLM RCA agent's reported skill survives honest evaluation, and
how much observability it actually needs. The answers: naming leaks alone were worth
57~points of fault-classification accuracy; with leaks closed, generic cross-modality
tooling still roughly doubles non-LLM baselines on localization at a cent per
incident; traces are ${\sim}20\times$ over-collected for this task; kernel telemetry
is an efficiency multiplier and the sole source of induced-DB-latency typing, but only
in \emph{interpreted} form --- raw kernel numbers are worse than none; and experience
layers help only as far as their selection is reliable. All claims ship with
machine-checkable no-leakage certificates over 500+ transcripts.
