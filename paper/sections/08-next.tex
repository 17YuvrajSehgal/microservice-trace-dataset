\section{What we propose to do next}
\label{sec:next}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Redesign the guide selector} --- the one component the data clearly
  indicts. We can iterate on it cheaply by replaying the 200+ recorded evidence
  briefings through candidate selectors without re-running any diagnoses.
  \item \textbf{Repeats for headline figures.} Whichever numbers we promote to
  headline claims get three-run repeats to tighten the noise band.
  \item \textbf{Cross-model replication} once a second provider key is available
  (one afternoon, a few dollars).
  \item \textbf{Write-up decisions.} Whether this becomes one study paper or a
  dataset paper plus a study paper, and for which venue --- the dataset-track
  deadline in early November is the anchor. The pre-registered fault predictions
  need their amendment log updated with the outcomes reported here.
  \item \textbf{Product track} (with our industrial collaborators' architecture):
  richer selection for the guide library, mining new guides automatically from
  recorded diagnoses, and exposing the system behind the interface layer their
  ecosystem expects.
\end{enumerate}

All raw materials for discussion --- every diagnosis record, the analysis scripts,
and the per-experiment archives --- are stored durably on the cluster and are
reproducible end to end.
