The idea

The agent decides what to collect
— from the problem itself.

Kernel-deep telemetry is terabyte-scale — you can’t capture everything, everywhere. Today’s tools dashboard what was already collected. Ours turns a plain-language problem into a scoped collection plan, then goes kernel-deep on only that.

The wedge: no incumbent (Datadog, Dynatrace) or research system (TMLL, HolmesGPT) decides what to collect from the problem statement — and none go kernel-deep.
